## The Challenge: Bridging the Skills Gap for Transformation

A leading UAE bank was executing a large-scale digital transformation across its **Retail** and **Corporate Banking** divisions. To achieve its transformation objectives, the client faced a critical challenge: rapidly identifying and onboarding specialized technical expertise that was not readily available internally.

The bank needed to quickly ramp up capabilities across infrastructure support and complex core banking technologies to keep the transformation on track.

---

## The Solution: Targeted and Rapid Talent Augmentation

Sphere IT assumed full responsibility for identifying talent gaps and augmenting the client’s teams with highly targeted skill sets. The strategy focused on accelerating delivery timelines by rapidly onboarding resources with the exact technical expertise required for immediate needs.

### Resource Onboarding Highlights

- **Core Transformation Skills**  
  Successfully onboarded **21 resources** with diverse skills and experience within **3 months**, supporting transformation initiatives across Retail and Corporate Banking in the UAE.

- **Niche Skill Sets**  
  Onboarded **18 specialized resources** within **3 months** to strengthen **Technology Services Management** and **Treasury** functions with critical niche expertise.

---

## Technology & Expertise Provided

The specialized support teams were instrumental in operating and maintaining complex enterprise technology platforms, including:

- **CALYPSO**  
  Calypso Senior Support Analyst, Calypso Support Analyst, SME Functional roles

- **SHAREPOINT & CRM**  
  SharePoint Support Analyst, MS Dynamics CRM Support

- **BPM (Business Process Management)**  
  BPM Support Analyst

- **FLUTTER**  
  Flutter Support Analyst

- **Core Banking & Switching Systems**  
  FIS Quantum & Ambit Focus, Vision Plus, IST Switch (Techno-Functional resources)

- **UNIX & Infrastructure**  
  UNIX, Storage & Backup Administrator

---

## The Impact: Optimized Infrastructure and Accelerated Projects

Through targeted talent augmentation, Sphere IT enabled the bank to achieve critical **Infrastructure Transformation Objectives**, forming the backbone of its broader digital strategy:

- Enhanced Active Directory services  
- Improved UNIX backup and storage capabilities  
- Desktop and application virtualization  
- Standardized and optimized end-user environments  
- Strengthened enterprise-wide network support  

By successfully bridging the skills gap, Sphere IT empowered the bank to accelerate complex transformation initiatives, strengthen its technology foundation, and enhance customer touchpoints through a more robust and specialized infrastructure.
