Fragmented back-office processes often slow down bank operations, leading to compliance risks, high costs, and lengthy development cycles for new workflows. Our client faced these exact challenges:

-	Slowed Approvals: Manual and fragmented back-office processes slowed operations and approvals.

-	Compliance Issues: Lack of visibility across departments led to compliance and tracking issues.

-	Long Deployment Cycles: New workflows required long deployment cycles due to traditional development methods.

-	High Costs: Repetitive tasks and limited automation resulted in high operational costs.

## The Solution: Low-Code BPM for Back-Office Agility
Sphere IT deployed a low-code automation solution designed to streamline back-office operations, reduce deployment time, and enhance process visibility across departments.

### Our strategic approach included:

-	Workflow Suite Deployment: We deployed a custom workflow suite to automate five key back-office functions: Core Operations, KYC, Cheque Requests, Card Capture, and Document Processing.

-	Centralization: All workflows were centralized within a low-code BPM environment for greater agility.

-	Continuous Delivery: We implemented DevOps pipelines for continuous integration and delivery.

### Technology & Implementation
The foundation for this high-speed automation was a modern, agile technology stack:

-	Low-Code BPM Platform: Enabled rapid configuration and orchestration of workflows.

-	Kubernetes Runtime: Supported blue-green deployments and zero downtime.

-	GitOps-Based CI/CD Pipelines: Ensured version control and automated delivery.

-	Audit-Grade Framework: Provided traceability and compliance readiness.

## The Impact
The implementation resulted in significant operational enhancements, cutting down delays and boosting process governance:

-	Processing Time Reduction: The client achieved a significant reduction in processing time across all back-office operations.

-	Enhanced Traceability: Traceability and compliance were enhanced through audit-ready workflows.

-	Accelerated Deployment: Accelerated workflow deployment cut release cycles from weeks to days.
By leveraging low-code automation and an agile DevOps approach, we transformed the client’s back-office from a source of delays into a highly efficient, transparent, and compliant operational engine.
